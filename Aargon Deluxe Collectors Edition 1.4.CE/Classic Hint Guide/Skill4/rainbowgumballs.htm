Level Notes


The level can be solved with one mirror left over. I'd like to note here that this level was hammered away at endlessly (as were many of the levels) by my friend der Mouse, who in a sense deserves partial credit for authoring many of these levels. They certainly were made better by his relentless testing. Stumping him (or trying to) was half the fun of trying to make these :-)
