Level The TNT Wall
Difficulty Easy
   
Author Steve Verreault
Pieces 2
   
   
   
Text Hints
Obscure "The right way is right"
Obscure "No need to catch them"
Good Hint "Watch the way slimes turn"
Great Hint "Make the slimes do a loop"
Level Notes


As with all of the other slime levels, you do not need to play quickly here. The trick is to figure out how the slimes move and then place your pieces in the right place and let them out.
