Level Notes


The name of this level actually comes from an ancient computer virus. It was called MYDOOM and had the effect of making your screen look "broken" by randomizing the character-to-glyph mapping (or "code page") of the display device. It did not actually cause any harm to your computer but once installed, getting rid of it was next to impossible because you couldn't really "read" the screen.
