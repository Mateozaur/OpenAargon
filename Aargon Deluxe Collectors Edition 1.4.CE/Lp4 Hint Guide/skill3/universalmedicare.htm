Level Notes


There is actually a kind of reason for naming the level this way. I suppose it's quite subtle, but in a way the solution represents something like a jab at the US "insurance-based" health care system where those with lots of money (green) get to stay (the green beam doesn't exit), and those with medium income (ie. blue[-collar]) get some attention just before getting kicked out and of course half of their support goes to help out our green friend. And red of course (emergency case, typically uninsured), well they get the "back-door" treatment: Sent out the back door and left to fend for themselves!
