Level Notes


I (Alan Carre) had such a hard time with this level that I decided to fix the position of the LBENDER. There are simply too many possibilities to explore when you can put it anywhere. I had it in the right spot of course, for a while. But since the solution was not forthcoming, I began to try different positions for it. So many new possibilities opened up I simply gave up on the level!
