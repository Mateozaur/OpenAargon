Level Notes


The original name of this level was called "the crusher". We happened to notice that the layout of the walls look a bit like a vertebra on a spine. So in order to turn this idea into a medically themed name, the idea of a spinal compression fracture came up, for which there is a fairly new operation which has the name of this level. The operation involves injecting a kind of cement into the weakened vertebra (usually caused by osteoporosis) to support the weight of the rest of the spine (kind of like having a tooth filled). As well a barrier is sometimes built on the outside to prevent further damage and supply more support (analogous to a dental crown). The beauty of this operation is that it is percutaneous meaning that access to the vertebra is done via needle-puncture, so the skin doesn't have to be cut. If you look at actual before and after X-ray images of this operation, in fact the "after" image looks very much like the solution to this level!
