Level Notes


This level is actually where the idea of prism cycling originated. We had noticed that the key to this level was to keep reorienting the "next" color on the appropriate path (after splitting) so as to be able to re-enter the prism for the next element of the output (which is also split). A mirrored prism (or even a regular prism) happens to do this automatically, and so is a much more efficient way to keep re-entering the prism from the right direction.
