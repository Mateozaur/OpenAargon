Text Hints
Step 1: "Using 4 free mirrors, turn the split beam into a box"
Step 2: "Rotate the lower rightmost mirror so that it becomes a blocker"
Step 3: "Move the same mirror up 2 squares"
Step 4: "Use rotate buttons or mouse with this mirror to send a beam right"
Step 5: "If there are only slimes in the lower box, bring the other mirror up"
Step 6: "Else keep toggling the box open and shut until only slimes in the box"
Step 7: "After all the TNTs have been eaten, return to start position"
Step 8: "Repeat this process up top"
Step 9: "After TNT's are all gone you may safely construct the solution"
