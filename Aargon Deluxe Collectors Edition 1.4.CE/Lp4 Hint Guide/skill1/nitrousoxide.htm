Level Notes


This level uses an advanced technique called "back-complimenting" (though it should be complementing) which is a method by which a primary beam's complement color can be created by refracting with a threeway first, and then splitting with another and then, within a loop formed from the resulting two beams, one inserts a color changer. This combines two out-going primaries neither of which, by definition, can be the original primary! Hence a white beam will form between the two threeways, and the complement color will emerge from the first threeway's 'output' side.
