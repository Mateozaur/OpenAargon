Level Notes


This level employs a unique design concept that has never been used before:

With two exceptions, every piece required to build the solution above also happens to be a blocker for the a white beam required to spin the coin above it's original position. So the mere act of moving these pieces into place has an important side effect; namely solving part of the puzzle!
