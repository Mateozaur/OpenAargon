Level Notes


This level introduces the "Mirrored" prism piece. The mirrored prism behaves just like a regular prism except it looks and behaves exactly as it's mirror image. For instance a white beam going up into the back of a prism would send the blue beam to the right. For the mirrored prism it would instead go left.

A bit of trivia about this level:
This level actually first appeared in the classic level set but with only regular prisms (Level: "Filter walls"). We found that by using mirrored prisms the same level could be completed with 2 fewer mirrors!

Clue: "An old beginning"

This clue refers to the fact that the first few moves in this level are the same as the first few moves in the classic level "Filter walls". It tells us that, assuming we remember:

"Split the beam with a prism and combine to make yellow just above the One Way piece".

Which is the way to begin solving "Filter walls".
