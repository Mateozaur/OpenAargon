Level Notes


This level's solution requires the use of a techinque called "Back complimenting", much the same way that the deluxe level "Back combining" requires the use the technique corresponding to it's level name. Here again, back reflection combined with color combination produces an open-ended beam of the desired color. With the deluxe "Back combining" level we started with white and split it into it's primary colors with a prism. We then reflected red and green back to create an open-ended yellow beam. In this case we do not start with white and no prism is used. In fact we start with blue and we are trying to create blue's "compliment color" which is yellow. Yellow is blue's compliment because it contains every primary color except blue. In terms of primary colors we can easily see this by writing down what we already know about white:

1. (RED + GREEN) + BLUE = WHITE ... or
2. YELLOW + BLUE = WHITE ... meaning
3. BLUE = WHITE - YELLOW

That is, if you take yellow away from white you are left with blue. Other compliment colors are easy to figure out in the same way:

1. compliment of RED = CYAN
2. compliment of GREEN = MAGENTA
3. compliment of BLUE = YELLOW

And, as expected the inverse is also true

1. compliment of CYAN = RED
2. compliment of MAGENTA = GREEN
3. compliment of YELLOW = BLUE

What about WHITE? Well the compliment of WHITE is simply BLACK (ie. no color at all). Conversely the compliment of BLACK is WHITE.
