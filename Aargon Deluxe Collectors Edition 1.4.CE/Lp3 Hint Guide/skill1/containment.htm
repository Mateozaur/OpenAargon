Level Notes


This level introduces the "Splicer" piece. The splicer is similar to the "Splitter" piece in that it splits the beam at right angles. The difference is that with the Splicer the original beam keeps on going! In a way the Splicer is like a mix between a "Splitter" and a "One Way" piece.
