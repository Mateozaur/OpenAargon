Level Notes


For those who do not already have level pack one:

This level introduces the selector piece (created by Gabor Vekony). The selector selects out a set of colored beams by deflecting them at a 45 degree angle, just like a refractor. The rest of the light continues to travel through the selector. The color of the light that would be deflected is indicated by the color of the selector. Note that if any light is sent through the selector 'backwards' it travels straight through without being deflected or filtered. Note as well a beam sent through through at an angle will always be stopped. That means that the selector does not work the same way backwards as forwards (ie. it is an asymmetric piece). Since the selector is asymmetric, it can be used in combination with mirrors for the purpose of 'back reflection' (similar to the threeway piece).
