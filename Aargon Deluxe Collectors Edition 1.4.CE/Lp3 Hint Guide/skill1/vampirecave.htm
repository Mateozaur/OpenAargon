Clue: "Natural prism"

This clue tells us to look for the most obvious location where a prism would be expected. Natural prism locations are places where you typically find 3 adjacent coins either in a straight line or a semi square with red, green and blue corresponding to the red/green/blue output from a prism's "face" side (the angled side). The coins need not be exactly red,green,blue but they must not be of a color that doesn't fit the prism pattern. For instance a magenta coin does not belong where green would normally come out (magenta does not contain green), but of course cyan would be ok (cyan does contain green).

If you haven't solved the level yet, can you spot this "Natural prism location"?
