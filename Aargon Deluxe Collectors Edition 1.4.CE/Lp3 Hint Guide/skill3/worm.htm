Level Notes


Again a seeminly simple level which tends to trip up a lot of people. This is certainly attributable to the placement of the white coin just above the laser source. It is very tempting to hit that coin and "try to deal with the rest later". Oddly enough (though my hearing is not so great) people appear to respond to this level by uttering the phrase "truck through" over and over again. I have yet to determine what this means or why they are saying it.
