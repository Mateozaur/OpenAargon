Level Notes


SPOILER WARNING! The text below contains the solution to a level from Level Pack One

Yet another excercise in prism filter combining. This one however appears to be the strangest of all. Combining three whites at different angles with a prism results in an outgoing white beam! This unusual convergence is quite difficult to spot and was first used in the level "Focal Point" from Level Pack One (Sci-Fi).
 

Cryptics: Warning: This section may contain spoilers


Clue: "When 1 and 1 and 1 is 1, cometh the Raven, for you have won!"

This clue merely refers to what's noted in the level notes above. In this case "1" refers to 1 white beam, and the solution to the level requires using prism filter-combining in order to combine 3 white beams to produce a single outgoing white beam. The outgoing beam hits the last coin and the 3 incoming beams are blocked by the prism thus solving the level.

So the clue is: "Combine 3 white beams with a prism to produce a white beam!"
