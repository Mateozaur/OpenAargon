Clue: "Waste not, want lot"

Normally the saying is: "Waste not, want not", meaning if you don't waste things then you will not be "for want". Here the word "not" has been replaced with "lot", meaning "lots" or "a lot". What this tells us is essentially the opposite of the usual saying: If we try to play the level with "minimal wastage" (ie. very efficiently) then we will "want lot", or in other words "lose". That means that the key to this level is to play a sequence of moves that appear to be (at least superficially) highly inefficient.

So the clue is: "The solution involves playing some seemingly-wasteful moves!"
