Clue: "Creepy filter walls"

The clue "Creepy filter walls" refers to the Classic level named "Filter walls" which is semi-duplicated in this set. The new (or Creepy) version of "Filter walls" is called "The other side". Hence "Creepy filter walls" can be translated to the phrase "The other side" which hints that the solution involves bringing one or more beams in from the right hand side.

So the clue is: "Bring one or more beams in from the right hand side!"
