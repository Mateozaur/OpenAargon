Clue: "From a puzzler's mantra, we compliment magenta around 1:30"

Another clue that, at first glance, probably appears to be nothing but gibberish. And again, this clue actually contains a lot of information. It's at least a "Great hint" and maybe even a "Spoiler"! So, if you want to solve this level on your own, stop reading now.

Ok, now let's decifer this clue. You may have already run into the first part in the Cryptics section for the level "Casket". If not, "a puzzler's mantra" is what people who love puzzles are always saying which is this: "Try to think outside the box". To come "From" a puzzler's mantra then, is to come from outside the box. This level contains a sort of box around it, the upper part being composed mostly of white filters. And apparently from there we are supposed to "compliment magenta around 1:30". Now the compliment color of magenta is green, and if 1:30 is a time, then a dial-hand clock would be pointing up and right (at 45 degrees) at exactly 1:30. So somehow we are supposed to create green, from outside the box by pointing the beam up and right. Well if we put a mirror there, and a green selector "inside" the box, and send the white beam in, a green beam will come out and hit the 2 green coins up top. So, this is not even really a clue, it is a detailed description of the first move!

So the clue is: "Point the white beam angled up-right and back-reflect into a green selector!"
