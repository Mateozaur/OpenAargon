Level Notes


This level started out as a 'concept' level. The concept was to design a simple-looking level such that if a particular coin is hit first, then the level cannot be solved. The special coin is chosen such that the desire to hit it is so great that the actual solution will almost certainly never be found. Of course, once a highly-skilled player has exhausted all possibilities they would eventually be forced to conclude (in the words of "Sherlock Holmes") that "Once you have discounted the impossible, then whatever remains, however improbably, must be the truth".

Caution though! There still exists some of the original level's sneakiness of providing false clues ;)
