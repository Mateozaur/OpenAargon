Clue: "Not the right distraction"

This one is really quite tough I think, even though the meaning may be fairly easy to surmize. As usual, because the word "right" can have two possible meanings we have 2 ways of interpreting the clue. One way is to think of "right" as meaning "correct" and the other as meaning "the direction right" (as opposed to "left"). Well I lied, there is in fact a third way to interpret this clue. And that is to think of "right" as having both meanings at the same time! If we look at it that way, then we can translate the clue as meaning "the non-left distraction is not the correct distraction". That is, if we're going to be distracted by something, it should not be whatever is on the right hand side. It should be something else. Well what's on the right hand side? Right ;)... I mean correct, it's those white coins. So we should not be distracted by those white coins! To not be distracted by those coins certainly implies we should not go after them right away... (err I mean "immediately"), but try to deal with the rest of the coins first.

So the clue is: "Don't go for the white coins first!"
