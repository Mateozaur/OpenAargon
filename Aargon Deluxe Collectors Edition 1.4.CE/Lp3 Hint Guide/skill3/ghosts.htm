Clue: "I select yellow to be the greatest color of all!"

This clue might seem like complete gibberish at first. It might even seem like somebody made a mistake! This is not the case and in fact it is a very good clue. There is enough information here to tell us what the first piece is, and probably even where to place it. So how is that possible? Well, to figure it out, ask yourself what the statement is rather than what it says. Yes, it is a compliment for "yellow". Well what's the compliment color for yellow? That's right, blue. The statement also includes the word "select" which almost certainly refers a "Selector" piece.

So the clue is: "Use a selector to extract blue from white!"

Note about the placement of the selector: Looking at the white beam you will notice that as blue is "selected out" it will be on a diagonal. Blue is required in order to get the white coins spinning so we will want to make sure it lies on the same diagonal as the two white coins. There are only a couple places to do that and it shouldn't take you long to find out which spot is the best.
