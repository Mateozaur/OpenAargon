Clue: "A level out-of-theme, and a crucial step forward"

This clue might be a tough one to figure out... What we know anyway is that whatever it means, it certainly refers to a very important component of this puzzle's solution. So let's look at the part we don't know about: "A level out-of-theme". What could this possibly mean? Think back over all of the levels in this set. In each and every case the levels had something to do with scary/spooky things correct? Well not exactly. It is true for all of the levels with one exception. Do you know which level I am talking about? If not, I'll save you the trouble and tell you: It was called "Back complimenting". There is nothing scary or spooky about that level, it is merely a tutorial level demonstrating the technique of back-complimenting.

So the hint is: "Use back-complimenting!"

I don't want to give it away, but I think you will eventually find yourself stuck for a yellow beam anyway. That should tell you which color you will need to back-compliment!
