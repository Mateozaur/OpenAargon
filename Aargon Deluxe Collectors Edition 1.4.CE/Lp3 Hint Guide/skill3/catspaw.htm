Cryptics: Warning: This section may contain spoilers


Clue 1: "A box on the left tells much of the tale"

This clue is meant to direct your attention to the lower-left 'box' with various pieces in it. These pieces cannot rotate. The fact that these pieces are unrotateable places significant restraints on how and where they can be used. So this is really not much of a hint, but it does direct your attention to this fact.

Clue 2: "But the draw of the paws tells the tale of the tail"

This is really a continuation of the first clue. Combining the two hints gives us the intended clue (or clues):

1: We can learn a lot by examining the orientations of the non-rotatable pieces
2: If we manage to get both "paws" spinning (the lower purple coins) then we will discover how to spin the upper left blue coin (ie. the tail coin)

Note as well that the 2nd hint tells us that if we do manage to get the paws spinning but that doesn't seem to show us how to get the tail spinning, then we've made a mistake!
