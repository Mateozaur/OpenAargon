Level Notes


This seemingly simple level is actually quite deceptive in a way. The placement of the polarizers suggests that the beam should pass through them at some point. This sort of misleading piece placement is not used very often, which makes it all the more effective as in this case. However, whenever a selector appears in a level we should really be looking out for what we call the "selector coin triplet". That is, if we see some coins arranged like this:

O x x
O y O

With O = coin and both x & y = "empty spot", you can bet that there will be a selector at position 'y' and that a beam will pass through the bottom two coins (from left to right) and then be back-reflected into the selector at 'y' somehow, thus causing the same beam to hit the top coin as well!
