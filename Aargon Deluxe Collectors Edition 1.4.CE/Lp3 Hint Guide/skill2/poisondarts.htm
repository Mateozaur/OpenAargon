Cryptics: Warning: This section may contain spoilers


Clue: "Sometimes "three" can be a four letter word"

This clue will not make sense until you try playing the level for a while. The expression "a four letter word" usually refers to a curse word (or vulgarity). So again, we have a clue which is play on words. In this "three" and "four" and a well-known expression. "Three" is in fact a five letter word, so we can be pretty certain about the expression! The hint is quite subtle but it should kick-in if you attempt what seems to be the most obvious first move which is to hit the most coins possible immediately, leaving the rest "for later". If you do that, you are in fact left with three coins to hit. You will have the following pieces remaining: a Splicer, a Threeway, and three mirrors. However hitting the remaining three coins, even with all those remaining pieces turns out to be impossible! So the hint really refers to those three coins which cannot be hit even with a tremendous arsenal of pieces to work with.

Continuing the hint it says "...other times it's just the opposite"

What that refers to, if you manage to realize the first mistake, is that the key to the level is to create three beams. This is done with a Splicer way down at the bottom. After that you use the three Threeways (yet 2 more 3's!) together with mirrors to hit all the coins.

So the two hints combine to tell you: "Make three beams, for use by three Threeways!"
