Clue: "Keep it stupid simple!"

This clue derives from the well-known saying: "Keep it simple stupid!" (or KISS) which is another way of saying "don't try be too complicated or clever, it's wiser to keep things simple". However, as you can see the phrase has been altered by swapping the words "simple" and "stupid. So what could this possibly mean? Well, part of the "meaning" comes simply from the fact that the phrase makes no apparent sense anyway. Another element of the clue is that it is apparently meant to express the opposite of the original. And lastly, disregarding the word "simple" the first 3 words tell us exactly what to do: namely something "stupid". The clue tells us basically that the solution to the puzzle will (at least initially) have the appearance of stupidity. So now it's just a matter of determining what the dumbest looking first move might be and to simply play it and see what happens. Well it's not really that hard to come up with the right "dumb-looking" move which is shown in the picture and text hints. Instead of trying to split the beam right away and go after the coins, it turns out that the best move is to "waste" 3 mirrors to send the white beam all the way up to the top right, and then come back down again. It sure looks dumb, but this level played any other way leads to failure!
