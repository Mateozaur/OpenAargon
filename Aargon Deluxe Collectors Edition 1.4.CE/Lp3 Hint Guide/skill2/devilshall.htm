Level Notes


This level actually originated from an error found in another level (Terror: Greenwitch badtime). After discovering that the latter level could be solved with four unused pieces remaining, we decided to play a joke on it's author by sending the level back to him (minus a few pieces) together with the note "I've found a more efficient way to solve the level". Not knowing the intended solution we expected that the he would attempt to re-solve the level in the intended way, but with fewer pieces (which we naturally assumed would be impossible). Eventually we informed our unfortunate friend of the joke and presented our solution which we knew was not intended and obviously unexpected. This level is in fact an exact duplicate of "Greenwitch badtime"s coin layout before it was repaired, forcing the intended solution. Again, unexpectedly we discovered yet again that even *more* pieces could be saved with the same layout (2 mirrors in fact). For this reason, and the complexity of the layout, we cannot be certain that the most optimal solution has already been found, which is why the "Crypic" clue says what it says. Yes, the clues will help you solve the puzzle one way but they certainly will not help you find a better way or even determine if such a better way exists. So if you are the kind of person who likes a real challenge, and hope to find yet another more efficient way to solve this level then the hints will not help you!
