Clue: "DV82XL"

This one is a little bit tricky: In a sense it is a puzzle in clue to a puzzle to a clue to a puzzle! And if the last sentence makes any sense to you then I bet you can figure it out on your own! However, that is not the purpose of this section so I will explain.

Look at the letters and numbers and say them out loud. Listen to yourself as you read it. Got it? Yes, it translates to this: "Deviate to excel". This is an old saying which normally means something like "Breaking with convention allows you to excel in life and achievements". So now the first puzzle is solved, we've decrypted the encrypted cryptic clue!

Now what does this saying have to do with this particular level? Well to "excel" we would assume to mean "to win" and to "deviate" would mean to veer away from what appears to be the path we should follow. To do that (to deviate) could mean several things, but given the fact that there are two prisms in this level, it would appear that in order to win we will have to break the yellow beam into red and green and then somehow put them back together again. Which is precisely what we need to do in order to win this level!
