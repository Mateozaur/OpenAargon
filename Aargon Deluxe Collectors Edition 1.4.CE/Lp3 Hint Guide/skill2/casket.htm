Level Notes


Strangely this seemingly simple level also seems to trip a lot of people up. With so few pieces and options one would think the answer would come quickly. Not so. The trouble with solving this level really does amount to the difficulty of "thinking outside the box" (which is where all the action actually occurs in this case). Once splicing one beam in, we are left with a second beam that may also be sent in! The answer is so simple that it is very easily overlooked.

Clue: "A classic puzzler's mantra"

This one should be easy. What is it that people who love puzzles are always saying you have to do? That is, what is their "mantra"? Do you know by now? If not, it happens to be the next clue which is: "Try to think outside the box".
