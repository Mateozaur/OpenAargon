Aaron Deluxe Demo Version Hints. Beginner's Beams Hints

Click on the grey areas to reveal the hints.

The hints are ordered so start with the top hint for each level, and work your way down.

Two Coins One Beam
Dont hit any coins with your first shot
First reflect the light right
Reflect right above or below both coins
Reflect the light up or down to solve it

Refract
Reflect first
Reflect below the two coins
Put the refractor down and to the left of the bottom coin

One Way Walls
Hit the top two coins first
Hit a single coin going down next
Go left next and hit one more coin
Go down and right to get the last 3 and solve it

Don't Cross
Don't let crossed beams hit the coins
use mirrors to block some beams
Use one mirror to block both a red beam and a green beam at the same time
Use the other mirror to block the far right green beam that's firing up

Split
The splitters are placed such that one is right next to the other
Your first split should hit the one coin on the left
Your second split should hit the other two coins

Threeway Split
The pointy end of the threeway faces up
Use the threeway to generate two more beams and not just refract
A single split can hit both coins on the diagonal

Banana Split
Split first using a regular splitter
Use two threeways 2nd
The two threeways go right next to the first splitter
Use a regular splitter to hit the left coins after the threeway
Split again with the threeway on the bottom left to solve it

Matching Colors
Hit white going down
Hit green going left
Hit Yellow on an angle
Hit purple going up
Hit cyan on an angle
Hit blue going left

Boom!
Place your mirrors first
Place one mirror above the left coin
Place one mirror below the right coin
Carefully split so that you hit the two mirrors

Double Mirror
Use the double mirror right in the middle
Reflect right through one one-way
Reflect down and left
Bring the beam back up and right to hit the double mirror again

Making Magenta
Red + blue = purple
Red goes down
Blue goes up

Yieldling Yellow
Red + green = yellow
Hit all the coins with red first
You should use exactly 6 mirrors to hit all the coins with the red beam
Use the other two mirror to make the green beam go back along the same path as red

Rotatorama
The top left red beam shoots southwest
The bottom left blue beam shoots northeast
The right blue beam shoots southeast
the bottom green beam shoots northeast

Mines
If you do this right there is never any danger of blowing up
Put a mirror in the path of the right white beam
Take the mirror out to trap the mine on the far right
Use the same mirror to reflect the left beam left and hit the coin
Wait for the timeout

3 Steps to Success
The name is a hint
You can solve the level in 3 moves
You can solve it such that you're never in any danger of blowing up
Like all these levels there is no speed required
MOve 1 : Put the extra mirror where the white beam crosses itself
MOve 2 : Put the top right mirror on the far top righthand corner spot
MOve 3 : Take the free mirror and reflect the beam right to hit the corner mirror

Filter
Hit the two green coins going down
Use the two blue coins going down
Use a green filter to stop the red beam from hitting one of the blue coins
Place the green filter two sqares above the two green coins in the path of the red beam
Reflect blue left and up to hit the other blue coin
Use the red filter to stop blue from hitting the green coin

Filter 2
You can rotate the lasers either left or right.
Use red to block green
Use blue to block red
use green to block blue
Get the green coin first
You'll need two filters to get green in place
set up a red and a blue filter to rotate green in place
Place the red filter directly below the green coin
Place the blue filter directly down and left of the green beam
Rotate the green beam left to hit the coin
Place the blue filter to the right and down from the red coin
Rotate the red beam in the proper direction to hit the red coin without blowing up
Do the same for the blue beam using the green filter to block it

Filter 3
Hit only 1 cyan coin on an angle
refract after hitting the first cyan coin and hit the second one going right
Reflect down
Hit the last blue coing going right and through the filter

Filter 4
You need to filter out the unwanted colors leaving only the desired color behind
The light on the bottom right tell you which colors each piece has. use them to help you.
Remember white = red+green+blue
Two filters can be used for each coin
For the red coin : Purple contains red so place a purple filter in that path
For the red coin : Yellow also contains red so place a yellow filter in that path
For the red coin : Purple takes out the green component and yellow takes out the blue component leaving you only with red
Do the same for the other coins.

Double Trouble
Filter each beam first
Reflect yellow down
Reflect purple up
The refractor gets used twice. Once for each coin.
Place a refractor in the center to hit both of the coins.

Prism 1
Use one prism to hit the blue coins
Use two prisms to hit the green coins
Use just a mirror to hit the red coins

Prism 2
You'll need to use one refractor twice to solve this level
Refract red up and to the right
Refract red up
Reflect red right to hit the two red coins
Use a prism to shoot the blue beam up into the same refactor red uses
The blue coins should already be hit if you used the right spot for the refractor
The refractor that's used twice goes 1 square directly to the left of the wood and on the same diagonal as the blue coins
Use 2 prisms to hit the green coins

Placement
There's one right spot the place the prism
Put the prism in the path of the white beam such that 3 colored beams come out. Now study how they come out. Where should the prism go to hit the coins?
You need to shift the white beam one square before hitting the prism with it
Reflect the white beam right one square and then up before hitting the prism

Two Prisms
The answer is symmetric.
Use the filters to prevent an explosion
The prisms go 2 squares away from the laser sources

Prism Merging
red+blue+green=white
The prism goes in the path of the red beam
Send green into the prism using one refractor - you should get a yellow beam
Send blue into the prism using one mirror - the beam coming out should be white
Reflect the white beam down to hit the coin

Split then merge
The name is a hint
use two splitters on the left hand side of the wall first
The first splitter should give you a blue beam
The second splitter sends the white beam up and down
Reflect the top beam to get a green beam
Reflect the bottom beam to get a red beam and hit the coin with red
Put the prism directly one square to the left of the coin
refract green into the prism
reflect blue into the prism
You should have white now

Merge then split
The name is a hint again
Make a white beam first
It should take only 3 pieces to make a white beam
reflect the white beam though the one-way
place a prism directly to the right of the one way
Use the last two mirrors to hit blue and green

Polarizers
Refract first
Reflect second
Refract directly above the laser source
Reflect down and right to hit the coin

Expert Polarizers
When you enter the box your beam should be purple inside
When you come out of the box you should be at an angle
Come out through a cyan polarizer
Reflect up and right to hit the blue coin

Easy Combination
Make a little cyan loop using green and blue first
Your cyan loop should use a refractor 1 square directly up and left from the two red coins
Reflect red down through the same refractor to win

Staircase
The direction of the green beam matters
Make the green beam come down and right not up and left
Remember that red's direction is unaffected by prisms
You'll have to go through some prisms when reflecting red
Reflect the red around in a spiral to hit the coins

Expert Combination
Split first
Then hit both prisms
The Easy combiantion level is kind of a hint for this one
Make a cyan loop just like the easy combination level
Double refract the blue from the top prism with the refractor in your cyan loop
Combine red and green by matching the red beam from one prism to the green beam from the other
Reflect red down and re-use the refractor you needed to make yellow
Match red which should be going through the one-way now with the left over blue beam to hit the purple coins

Diagonal Trouble
Ask yourself where you want to end up
The last coin hit is the blue coin
Don't start on the blue diagonal
Loop around twice

Cross Corner
The answer is symmetric
Combine colors with a threeway
The first pieces used are the refractors
Next use a mirror then a splitter
The slit-mirrors go near the laser sources

Roundabout
The name is a hint
The first piece used for magenta is a refractor
You can't get yellow with yellow
Split Magenta with a prism in the lower-right corner
Split yellow with the other prism to get the yellow coin spinning
