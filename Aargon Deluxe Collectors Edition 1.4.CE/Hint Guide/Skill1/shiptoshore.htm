Level Notes


A nautically themed level. It would have been a good one for level pack 2 had I known at the time that it would have a nautical theme. I wrote the name using symbols the way a hacker might because I'd just added things like ! marks and numbers to the game font. The number 5 is an S, 0 is the letter 0, and ! is the letter i. The number 2 in the middle is read as it sounds and means "to".
