Level Kosovo
Difficulty Overload
   
Author Gabor Vekony
Pieces 2
   
   
   
Text Hints


Obscure Left is good.
Good Hint One laser can be used to block 3 barrels.
Great Hint Get all the mines to the left hand side.
Good Hint Step1 : Eat the 3rd row leftmost barrel with the slime.
Good Hint Step2 : Block the 2nd row leftmost barrel from the mine with a mirror.
Good Hint Step3 : Using the other mirror: Block the green beam going up to let one of the bottom mines go left.
Good Hint Step4 : Reusing the same mirror : Block the white and green beams at the same time and trap him on the far left bottom corner.
Good Hint Step5 : Repeat steps two and 3 trapping the other bottom mine on the left but in the chamber above the bottom left hand corner.
Good Hint Step6 : Block the red beam and let the two remaining mines down to where the first 2 were.
Good Hint Step7 : Repeat steps 2 and 3 and trap one of the mines in another of the chambers. Note that you might have to block one of the barrels to do this safely using a mirror.
Good Hint Step8 : Here is the tricky part. Using the bottom rightmost green laser; block the top 3 barrels making a fourth chamber at the top. You can now have all 4 mines trapped to the left of the white beam.
Good Hint Step9 : Now that the mines are all trapped on the left... Rotate all the color lasers to hit the coins.
