Text Hints
Good Hint Step 1 : Place a prism and make a purple square with a green line going through it.
Good Hint Step 2 : Repeat step one until the mine is on the top left of the green line.
Good Hint Step 3 : Place a mirror to block the red and blue beams at the same time.
Good Hint Step 4 : Remove a mirror and let the blue go across.
Good Hint Step 5 : Place a mirror so that red will be reflected down before hitting the righthand barrels.
Good Hint Step 6 : Let red go across and down.
Good Hint Step 7 : Make a new purple loop the length of the level.
Good Hint Step 8 : Remove the prism turning the long purple loop into a long white loop..
Good Hint Step 9 : Put the prism back in on the top line so that the green beam is lined up to hit the green coin and the blue beam is blocked by a filter. The mine should be trapped on the right hand side.
Good Hint Step 10 : Hit the green coin and you are done.
