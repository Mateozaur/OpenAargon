Level Notes


One of the early black coin levels. We thought initially that black coins were just like walls. That is, that there was no difference in terms of gameplay. A black coin is very similar to a wall in that a laser can't travel along that path. Black coins are a little different though because unlike a wall a laser can't end on a black coin to win the level. They were really meant to replace exploding barrels on levels where we wanted to kill certain paths to stop alternate solutions, but felt the level was hard enough without the extra difficulty that having a few barrles around adds.
