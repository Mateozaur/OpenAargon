Text Hints
Obscure There are many ways to solve this.
Obscure These hints describe one safe way to approach the level.
Good Hint Step 1 : Place a refractor rotated to send the green beam right across the white coin.
Good Hint Step 2 : Place another refractor rotated to block the green beam just before the laser edge on the right hand side.
Good Hint Step 3 : Place a prism in the top left corner such that the refractors send the green beam across the white coin and the box around the level is purple.
Good Hint Step 4 : If the mines are not trapped in the top righthand portion of the enclosure repeat step 3 until they are. Should be easy to time.
Good Hint Step 5 : Rotate the bottom right mirror so the purple becomes blue and red beams ending on that mirror. This is to stop red going around.
Good Hint Step 6 : Block the blue beam with a prism. This frees up the bottom left mirror.
Good Hint Step 7 : Use a mirror to send the red beam to the left and hit the same blocker used to block the green beam.
Good Hint Step 8 : Remove the blocker leaving you with a yellow enclosure.
Good Hint Step 9 : Put the refractor as a blocker right next to the prism giving you back seperate red and green beams.
Good Hint Step 10 : Remove the prism such that the white beam follows the same enclosure path as the yellow did and ends on the refractor as a blocker.
Good Hint The white beam hits the coin and the mines are blocked. You should be all done.

Level Notes


This level has many many approaches. The one described above is a safe way of doing it, in that, at no time are you at risk of blowing up from free mines. For instance, as an example of an unsafe way to solve the level, it is possible to complete the level using only 2 refractors and a little timing to make sure the mines are in the top right hand portion of the screen.
