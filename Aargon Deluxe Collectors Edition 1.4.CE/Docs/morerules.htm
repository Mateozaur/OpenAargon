A Few More Rules You Must Know


1. Exploding Barrels

You must not hit these with any beams of light or allow floating mines (see in moving items section) to hit these either. If you hit one you will have to restart the level.

2. Crossed beams are not considered merged

If you have two beams of light from different directions crossing on a square that square is not considered to be the crossed color. In fact crossing is altogether useless since you are not allowed to make a coin spin with cross beams.

3. Pieces can be swapped

If you drag a piece onto another piece that can also be moved the two pieces will swap places. This is useful when solving some levels.
