Glossary of Terms

Brick - An item that does not allow light to pass through it.
Clone - To place a piece that is identical to the selected piece under the cursor.
Coin (Colored) - The items that spin when it hit by a single laser beam of the same color.
Coin (Black) - The items that spin when no laser beam hits them.
Composite Beam - The resulting laser beam when two beams of differing color are combined.
Erase - To place a blank piece under the cursor.
Laser- An item generates laser light.
Locked Level- A level that cannot be accessed because not enough previous levels have been solved.
Moveable- An item that can be moved to a different location during play.
Paint- To place a piece of a specified type under the cursor.
Piece- Any object that can appear within the playing area (including blanks).
Rotateable - An item that can be rotated during play.
Selected Piece - The item that has the yellow highlight box surrounding it.
Solution - Any configuration of items that wins the level.
Swap - The act of exchanging two pieces in the playing area.
System Complete - The state of the game immediately after a level is solved (all coins are spinning).
Win a Level - To have all of the coins in the level spinning simultaneously without causing an explosion.
Wood - An item that does not allow light to pass through it (similar to Brick).
