What's new in Aargon Deluxe?

Many things have changed in Aargon Deluxe. It is not a minor upgrade but a complete new version. We tried to include everything in this version that we could not in the original. We also tried to include as many of the suggestions we received from players of Aargon (classic? :-)).

Here is a list of changes to look for...

New graphics; Almost all the graphics with a few small exceptions have been redone from scratch

New Levels; Aargon deluxe comes with a set of 120 new levels of varying difficulty. In general they are harder than the original set.

New Pieces; There are 3 new gameplay pieces in Aargon Deluxe. The slit double mirror, the three way splitter merger, and the color converter.

Undo and Redo; Undo and Redo buttons were added to allow you to undo a move or redo a move. They can also be used while editing your own levels

Windowed modes supported; You can now alt-tab out of and back into Aargon Deluxe as well as play Aargon Deluxe in windowed mode

Individual player tracking; Many players can track their progress individually on the same computer and for different sets of levels

Customized Pieces and Maps; This isn't immediatly apparent but Aargon deluxe was modified in such a way that we can bring you new pieces and new level packs more easily. So expect to see more upgrades in the near future!

New sound and music; We've completely re-worked the sound effects and music.

Tutorial Mode; Aargon is a difficult puzzle game so a tutorial mode was added to help players learn the game and make it easier for new players when starting
