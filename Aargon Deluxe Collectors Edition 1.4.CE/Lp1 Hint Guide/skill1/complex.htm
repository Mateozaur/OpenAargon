Level Notes


This level introduces the "Complex" piece. The "Complex" is similar to the selector piece in the following way:
When light enters the back of the complex, some colors are deflected out at a 45 degree angle like a refractor.

However, the complex is not the same as the selector because when light is sent back into the complex in reverse, the piece acts symmetrically. In that respect, the complex works in the same manner as other symmetric color-deflecting pieces (such as the prism piece). For instance, if light is sent straight into the complex in reverse, the complex's deflected colors are filtered out. But if light is sent in reverse into the complex along the 45 degree angle where it deflects, then the complex's non-deflected colors are filtered out. If you think about it, this is exactly the same behaviour as a prism piece.

To make it easier to see what colors would be filtered and deflected, the complex's color is shown on the outside of the piece, and the filtered color is shown inside.
