Level Notes


This level introduces the "Spaceship" moving piece. You may find the spaceship confusing at first so I will take some time here to describe how it works.

1. Spaceship Movement
The spaceship moves a little bit like the ships in the old space invader's game. It moves, generally, from left to right, or right to left, and then at some point, goes down 'one square'. More specifically, it will try to visit every square on one row before moving down to the next. However, if it sees a black hole (the whirpool) it will fall into it and disappear forever, unless the black hole is being hit by laser light. If a black hole is being hit by laser light then it becomes disabled and looks like a wall as far as the spaceship is concerned. The spaceship can see black holes through laser beams, but not through other pieces. If it sees two black holes, and one is closer, it will fall into the closer one. And lastly, once it's been sucked towards a black hole, it forgets all about where it's been before.

2. Spaceship Operations
For its operations, it will crash into crystals or fuel cells (the blue pulsing things) and blow up the level. It can, however, shoot bullets. It can shoot bullets when it's charged up. To get it to charge up, make it go over top of a fuel cell and it will tractor the cell to charge up. Once it's charged, it will have a glowing force field surrounding it and it will be able to shoot one shot at a crystal or fuel cell making the obstacle dissapear. Note that it only shoots in the direction it's moving in.
